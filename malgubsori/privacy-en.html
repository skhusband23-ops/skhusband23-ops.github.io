Hoofbeats Privacy Policy

App: Hoofbeats (말굽소리) · Developer: SKHB · Effective October 30, 2026 · Contact skhusband23@gmail.com · 한국어

Hoofbeats (the "app", shown as 말굽소리 in Korean) is a diary for horse riding lessons and trail rides. This document explains what information the app collects and why, where and how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. Every feature of the app is free and there are no in-app purchases.

2. Where information is processed (processors) and international transfer

The app does not run its own servers; it uses the Google services below. Each time you use the app, information is sent over an encrypted connection (TLS) to Google data centers (in the United States and elsewhere) and stored there.

If you do not want your information transferred abroad, you can choose not to sign up, or delete your account. In that case the record-saving features cannot be used.

3. Information kept only on your phone

Your color theme, save-sound setting, next lesson date, part of the recent trail-route archive and records not yet uploaded to the server are also stored on your phone. To save space, the trail archive keeps mostly recent rides and clears the oldest ones automatically. Uninstalling the app removes the information on your phone, but records on the server remain until you delete your account.

4. How to delete your information

  • A single record: open the record and delete it. The record, its attached photo and its route are removed from the server together. Routes left in the GPS Archive on the Trail screen can be deleted separately in the archive.
  • Your whole account: Home → My profile (top right) → Delete account → confirm your password. The account, records, photos, routes and profile on the server and the records on this phone are deleted immediately and cannot be restored. Without the app, you can also request deletion by email as described in the account and data deletion guide (in Korean; an email to the address above in English is fine). Requests are handled within 30 days at the latest.
  • Ads: in your phone's Google settings → Privacy → Ads, you can delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads. In regions where consent is requested, you can change your choice at any time under My profile → Ad privacy settings.

5. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop its processing, at any time. You can edit or delete records directly in the app, and download your records as a file from My profile → Export records. For any other request, email the contact address below and we will handle it without delay after verifying your identity.

6. How we keep it safe

  • All transfers use an encrypted connection (TLS).
  • Access rules on the server allow only the logged-in owner to read and write their records.
  • The developer cannot see your password; Google stores it encrypted.

7. Location

Before requesting location permission, the app first explains the purpose when you start recording a trail ride and asks for your agreement. Location is used only to build your trail ride record, and the app does not pass your location to advertisers or other companies (the ad SDK estimating an approximate region from your IP address is as described in section 1). You can turn off location permission at any time in your phone's Settings → Apps → Hoofbeats (말굽소리) → Permissions.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect information from children under 14. If we learn that such information has been stored, we delete it immediately.
